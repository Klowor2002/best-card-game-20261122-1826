EMBERFALL

DUEL OF THE EMBER PACT

Two ember mages. Three burning lanes. One pact, sealed in fire. Outwit Vex the Ash Seer before your own flame gutters out.

Mana — grows by 1 each turn, up to 10. Spend it to play cards.
Creatures — played into a lane; at turn's end they strike the foe across from them, or the enemy mage.
Spells — fireballs, mending flames and ember draws. Click a card, then its target.
Victory — reduce Vex to 0 health. Lose yours, and the pact claims you.

An original card game · no account, no network, no mercy

VICTORY

THE PACT IS YOURS

Vex's flame dies to an ember. The lanes cool, the pact burns your name into the ash.

DEFEAT

THE EMBER CLAIMS YOU

Your flame gutters out. Vex gathers the ashes — and waits for a rematch.
